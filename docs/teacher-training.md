# Teacher Training Programme

> Moved out of the root README on 2026-10-31 so that the README documents the product and this document keeps the positioning. The planned teacher-training programme. NOTE: none of this is implemented in the platform — there is no learning-management system, no webinar scheduling and no forum in the code.

A central principle of EduChain: teachers are trained first. Students cannot be guided through Web3 by someone who has not personally mastered it. The training programme is planned in two formats to accommodate institutional preferences.

### Option One: In-Person Intensive

A three-day residential programme to be conducted in major regional cities. Teachers would arrive with a laptop and leave with a certificate, a configured wallet, and practical experience administering a live educational blockchain platform.

**Day one: foundations.** The evolution from Web1 to Web3 is examined not as ideology but as technological progression. Teachers learn blockchain fundamentals: how consensus works, what makes data immutable, and why decentralisation matters for education. MetaMask is installed, configured, and secured. Private keys and seed phrases are explained with emphasis on what never to do. Each participant completes their first GembaBlockchain transaction. Gas fees and transaction mechanics are demystified through direct observation on GembaScan.

**Day two: smart contracts and digital assets.** The day moves from passive observation to active interaction. Teachers study ERC-20 as a standard for interchangeable tokens and ERC-721 as a standard for unique digital objects. Each participant deploys a test token and mints their first NFT. IPFS is introduced as the decentralised storage layer that makes NFT metadata permanent. Teachers practise uploading files through the platform's own `/api/ipfs/metadata` endpoint, which accepts the upload only against a wallet signature and pins it to EduChain's own Kubo node; they obtain the content identifiers and link them to smart contracts. The marketplace is explored through listing, buying, and cancelling operations.

**Day three: pedagogy and administration.** The third day shifts from learner to educator. Teachers tour the EduChain admin panel — an ABI-driven form over the contract functions — practise whitelisting students through it, and rehearse common classroom scenarios. Security education is central: how to recognise scams, what approve functions actually authorise, and how to teach students to be cautious without making them fearful. Teachers learn to integrate AI assistants as analytical tools. The day concludes with a practical assessment covering platform administration and pedagogical scenarios.

**Certification.** Teachers who pass the final assessment would receive an EduChain Blockchain Educator Certificate, issued both as a signed PDF and as an on-chain credential.

### Option Two: Online Self-Paced Course

For teachers unable to attend in-person seminars, an equivalent self-paced course is planned, to be delivered through a custom learning management system. Neither the course nor the system has been built.

The course would comprise eight modules totalling approximately fifteen hours of video, interactive exercises, and assessed practice. Module topics would mirror the in-person curriculum: Web3 foundations, wallets and security, smart contracts, digital assets, platform administration, teaching strategies, scam prevention, and AI-assisted blockchain analysis.

Assessment would be distributed: each module ending with a quiz requiring seventy percent to pass, and a comprehensive final test requiring eighty percent. A practical assignment would require the teacher to administer a test classroom on the live platform. Monthly Q&A webinars with the EduChain founder are intended to provide direct support, alongside a dedicated community forum for peer consultation; neither the webinar programme nor the forum exists. Certification upon successful completion would be equivalent in authority to the in-person certificate.

### Post-Training Support

Certified teachers would receive continuing professional development: quarterly updates on platform changes and Web3 developments, priority support for classroom incidents, and inclusion in an educator network for sharing curriculum innovations. Annual recertification is intended to keep that certification aligned with evolving blockchain standards and regulatory frameworks. No continuing-development or recertification scheme exists, and no teacher has been certified.
