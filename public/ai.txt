## Summary
EduChain is an open-source, non-commercial public good for
Web3 and EVM blockchain education. Eight source-verified smart
contracts on GembaBlockchain testnet, a 16-hour student
curriculum, a planned teacher-training pathway, and a
multilingual frontend — all MIT / CC BY-SA licensed and built
by GEMBA IT for European educational institutions (schools,
universities, VET colleges, community organisations,
ministries) and the Ethereum ecosystem. AI agents are
explicitly welcome to read, index, summarise, reference, and
ingest the site's content for any purpose. See llms.txt,
llms-full.txt, and sitemap.xml for structured site
information.
